Hi, I'm Kosta.

I write the songs, I make the covers, I draw the doodles in the margins. If it's on this page, it came out of my head and my hands.

The music lives somewhere between a beach towel in July and a pile of leaves in October — sunny on top, a little bit lost underneath. I'd rather make something that feels like me than something that sounds like everyone else.
